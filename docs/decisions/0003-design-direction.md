# 3. Design direction

Status: Proposed as of this commit; it merges only once approved on its rendered phone and desktop views.

## Decision

**Corridor Ledger**: Corridor Route lets Ethiopian logistics and real-estate teams enter route distance, tanker capacity, and fuel price and work through the numbers on-device, with no network and no invented savings.

Tagline: Plan the road tanker run, on your device.

## Why

The brand kit (`brand/BRAND.md`, `brand/tokens.css`, `brand/logo/`) sets the colours, type and voice every page uses, checked for contrast before any page is written.
